Privacy Policy – TypeTempo

Last updated: September 2026

TypeTempo is a private, on-device typing trainer. Your tests, passages, coins, and shop unlocks stay on this phone. We do not create an account or upload typing data to our servers.

Data stored on device

  • Test history, saved passages, language, duration, and speed goal
  • Coins, owned shop items, and the active look
  • Purchase tokens needed to restore Remove ads and TypeTempo Pro

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – coin packs, Remove ads, and TypeTempo Pro (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N277.json to read the disable field (no personal data is sent)

We do not sell personal data. Contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
